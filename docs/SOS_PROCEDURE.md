## 2. Target response times

- Acknowledge an SOS within: `client to supply` minutes.
- First contact with the nurse within: `client to supply` minutes.
- Escalate after: `client to supply` minutes.

## 3. Responder steps

1. Acknowledge the alert through the approved secure link or supported SMS reply path.
2. Call the nurse. If there is no answer, follow the approved contact procedure: `client to supply`.
3. If danger is confirmed or the nurse is unreachable, contact emergency services using the owner-verified numbers: `client to supply`, and share the location link only when authorised and appropriate.
4. Send the nearest available colleague or supervisor if appropriate and safe.
5. Keep the alert open until the nurse confirms they are safe or the outcome is recorded.

## 4. After the incident

- Record the outcome and response notes in the alert.
- Management incident report within: `client to supply` hours.
- Review lessons and update this procedure.

## 5. Drills

- Drill frequency: `client to supply`.
- Use test mode and the owner-approved test number only.
- Record acknowledgement time, contact time, escalation steps, outcome and corrective actions. Never use a real person's emergency contact for an unannounced test.

Names, roles, hours and verified phone numbers: `client to supply`.

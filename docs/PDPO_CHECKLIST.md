# PDPO registration and compliance checklist

Organisation tasks for the business owner, with developer support. This is not legal advice. Confirm current forms, fees and requirements on the official PDPO portal: https://pdpo.go.ug/register.

## A. Decide who the data controller is

- [ ] Name the legal entity that owns the nurse-app data: `client to supply`.
- [ ] Record the developer's role (processor acting for the controller, or controller) in a written agreement.

## B. Gather before applying

- [ ] Corporate email for the portal account: `client to supply`.
- [ ] Describe personal data processed: nurse identity/contact details, client display name, client locations, GPS coordinates at check-in/out, timestamps, visit photos, consent records and audit records.
- [ ] State the purpose of processing in plain language: `client to supply`.
- [ ] Name the data protection officer/contact: `client to supply`.
- [ ] Record storage/processing locations and cross-border transfers for the actual MongoDB provider/region, Cloudinary, Vercel, Firebase (if enabled), and other configured vendors.
- [ ] Approve an information-security/data-protection policy covering access control, encryption, backups, incident response and retention.
- [ ] Approve retention periods and verify the scheduled retention implementation before enabling real client data.
- [ ] Complete the breach-response procedure and confirm who contacts the PDPO.

## C. Apply

- [ ] Create the portal account and verify the email.
- [ ] Submit the current required application and cross-border documents, as advised by the PDPO.
- [ ] Confirm current fees on the official portal before payment.
- [ ] Respond to PDPO queries.
- [ ] Store the registration certificate in the approved compliance repository.

## D. After registration

- [ ] Diary annual renewal and annual compliance reporting requirements.
- [ ] Review registration when system purpose, data categories, or storage locations change.
- [ ] Keep privacy notice, consent records, retention logs and breach register up to date.

## E. Legal review required before go-live

- [ ] Controller/processor roles and client consent wording reviewed by a Ugandan lawyer.
- [ ] Cross-border processing and vendor arrangements reviewed.
- [ ] No real nurse or client data is entered until registration status, privacy notice, consent, retention, database tier and restore-drill readiness are confirmed.
